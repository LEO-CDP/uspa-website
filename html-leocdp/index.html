LEO Philosophy

Context → Decision → Outcome

LEO is not just a place to store customer data. It is a decision layer that connects business identity, expertise, customer persona understanding, action, and measurable outcomes.

Identity

Define who you are, what you stand for, and the business context in which you create value.

Expertise

Connect domain knowledge, capabilities, products, and accumulated experience to customer needs.

Customer Persona Understanding

Build dynamic, evidence-based personas instead of static demographic profiles.

Decisions

Use data, context, persona state, and AI reasoning to identify what should happen next.

Actions

Turn decisions into campaigns, workflows, recommendations, conversations, and operational actions.

Outcomes

Capture results as evidence and feed them back into the next decision cycle.
